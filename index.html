What You Will Be Doing

Our client built an AI system that turns speech into text. Some recordings were too difficult for it — unclear speech, background noise, or people talking over each other. Your job is to do what the AI could not: listen to a short clip and write down exactly what was said, along with a few labels that describe what you heard. Your work teaches the system to handle difficult audio better.
